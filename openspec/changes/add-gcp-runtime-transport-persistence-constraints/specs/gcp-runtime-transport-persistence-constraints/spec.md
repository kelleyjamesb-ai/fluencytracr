## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Machine-distinct constraints-only identity

The contract SHALL identify as `SECTION_7_5A_CONSTRAINTS_AND_OPEN_OBLIGATIONS_NOT_FULL_SECTION_7_5`, set all five non-substitution flags false, classify `canonical_runtime_qualification_item=false`, `qualification_contract_set_member=false`, and `qualification_path_dependency=false`, and use only `SECTION_7_5A` domains. It SHALL NOT satisfy full Section 7.5, any parent positive slot, any runtime approval, or the Section 7.6 dependency prerequisite.

#### Scenario: Constraints hash is submitted for parent approval

- **WHEN** any actor supplies the Section 7.5A hash to a Section 7.3 or Section 7.4 full-Section-7.5 slot
- **THEN** parent admission SHALL resolve the bytes and reject the Section 7.5A schema/kind/domain regardless of approver input

### Requirement: Exact provider provenance without live claims

The contract SHALL bind 53 sanitized official public-document sources, the exact external archive identity, inherited Section 7.3 KMS source identity, and the 89-row method research inventory. Public source bytes SHALL remain external restricted provenance. Literal method replay SHALL NOT imply semantic classifier completeness, live configuration, delivery completeness, or authority.

#### Scenario: Method literal exists but applicability is unresolved

- **WHEN** a method string replays from exact source bytes but principal route, permission, log type, resource, or Policy Denied applicability is unclosed
- **THEN** the contract SHALL retain the semantic-classifier prerequisite as `OPEN_BLOCKING`

### Requirement: Exact inherited obligations remain unsatisfied

The contract SHALL bind the ten Section 7.2 policy fields, Section 7.3 roles/operations/ten-key positive interface and empty approvals, and the exact current Section 7.4 bytes with all sixteen approval arrays empty. The nine Section 7.4 acceptance nodes plus token-freshness timestamp feed SHALL remain `UNSATISFIED_BLOCKING`.

#### Scenario: Parent approval array changes

- **WHEN** any Section 7.4 approval array differs from exact empty state or parent bytes differ
- **THEN** Section 7.5A parent projection validation SHALL fail and require a new reviewed contract version

### Requirement: Negative constraints are fail-closed

The contract SHALL freeze network/channel, local ephemeral, GCS, Spanner, audit/Logging, privacy, and authority constraints. It SHALL prohibit workload persistence egress, relayed quote transport, payload logging/persistence, false atomic-list/batch claims, Section 7.6 attempt semantics, method-filtered audit universes, Policy Denied exclusions, sensitive evidence projection, and additive authority.

#### Scenario: Required Policy Denied event can be excluded

- **WHEN** any required inherited or internal method lacks total Policy Denied applicability, route, origin, or full-interval no-exclusion proof
- **THEN** audit completeness SHALL remain held

### Requirement: Open-prerequisite graph is total

The contract SHALL contain exactly `S75A-P00` through `S75A-P19`, each `OPEN_BLOCKING`, and the exact bidirectional edge graph for every constraint/interface. Unknown, missing, extra, orphaned, or asymmetric edges SHALL reject compile closure.

#### Scenario: One prerequisite is removed from the reverse graph

- **WHEN** a forward edge remains but its exact reverse edge is absent
- **THEN** prerequisite-registry composition SHALL reject

### Requirement: Compile-only hashes cannot become runtime evidence

The contract SHALL use the exact twelve-node acyclic `SECTION_7_5A` hash DAG and no live/evidence/policy/approval/record/checkpoint/result node. Source, review, and contract hashes SHALL have `authority_effect=NONE` and SHALL be prohibited from runtime-approved registries.

#### Scenario: Contract hash is relabeled as a verified record

- **WHEN** a Section 7.5A hash is inserted into an opaque-record, approved-contract, verified, replay-ready, or runtime-approved slot
- **THEN** non-substitution validation SHALL reject

### Requirement: Mandatory HOLD and future readiness boundary

The v1 recorded result SHALL be `SECTION_7_5A_CONSTRAINTS_CLOSED_OPEN_PREREQUISITES_BLOCK_LIVE_INTERFACE_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY_HELD`. Section 7.5A SHALL contain no SUT and cannot receive `READINESS_GO`. A later full Section 7.5 SHALL require a separate human-created high-risk queue item and the complete readiness protocol before SUT work.

#### Scenario: Section 7.5A tasks are complete

- **WHEN** every docs-only Section 7.5A artifact and review gate passes
- **THEN** full Section 7.5, Section 7.6 eligibility, implementation, external action, and runtime authority SHALL remain blocked
